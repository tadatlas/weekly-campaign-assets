A note from Michael Coombs

Where I’m seeing opportunity

Some of the most compelling opportunities in today’s market are in homes that would be difficult to recreate.
Across Atlas, we’re seeing sales come together as buyer and vendor expectations become more closely aligned. Buyers are taking a considered approach, but there’s still genuine interest in quality properties.
In today’s market, it’s worth looking beyond the asking price. Location, build quality and lifestyle all contribute to a home’s value, and for buyers who recognise these qualities, there are opportunities worth considering.
Market perspective

The value of what’s already built

Take 9 Sirius Avenue and 9 Burrawong Avenue in Mosman. Both reflect an extraordinary investment in architecture, construction and craftsmanship.
Burrawong alone took six years to create — two years of planning and four years of construction.
With today’s building costs, buyers considering a new build or major renovation have more to weigh up than the purchase price. There’s the cost of construction, the complexity of the process and the years involved before a home is ready to live in.
For some buyers, securing a completed home means making compromises on certain preferences. But the quality, time and investment already behind that home can make it a compelling proposition.
It’s worth considering not only what a property costs today, but what it would take to achieve the same result.
Market outlook

Looking ahead

My expectation is that the market will remain relatively steady over the next 12 months, without dramatic movement in either direction.
What I’m watching closely is the availability of quality stock, which I expect to remain limited next year, particularly in tightly held locations.
There are still buyers watching the market closely, even if they’re not all ready to act. When sentiment returns, I expect competition for the best homes to increase quickly.
For those not yet financially ready, waiting makes sense. But for buyers in a position to act, particularly those buying and selling in the same market, there are opportunities worth considering while expectations are aligned with current conditions.
It’s not about trying to predict the bottom of the market. It’s about recognising the right property, understanding its value and making an informed decision.
